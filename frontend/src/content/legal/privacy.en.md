## 1. Data We Process

- Phone number (to sign in with a one-time code and protect your account).
- Profile data: display name, username, avatar, linked Telegram handle (if provided).
- Usage and chat data: chat list, roles/permissions, settings, events (e.g., joining via invite link).
- Content you create and upload: messages, attachments/files, media.
- Payment and account data: subscription status, technical payment identifiers/events (we do not store full card details).
- Technical data: IP address, user agent, request timestamps, error and security logs.

## 2. Purposes

We process data to:

- provide the Service functionality (chats, messaging, file uploads);
- authenticate users (send and verify one-time codes);
- maintain security (prevent abuse and investigate incidents);
- handle subscriptions and access control;
- respond to support requests and improve the Service.

## 3. Legal Bases

Depending on applicable law, legal bases may include:

- performance of a contract (the Terms of Service);

- Operator’s legitimate interests (e.g., security and abuse prevention);

We may use service providers (processors), including:

## 5. Retention

We retain data as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, and ensure security. Backups may be kept for a limited period.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical measures, including access controls, least privilege, logging, and secure data transmission.
